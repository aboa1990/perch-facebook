Data Deletion Instructions

PERCH Poster, an app of Perch.mv. Last updated: October 2026.

PERCH Poster does not collect or store personal information about Facebook users, Page visitors, or customers. It only handles product information from the Perch.mv store in order to publish posts on the Perch.mv Facebook Page.

If you want data removed

If you think the app holds any information about you, send an email to abo.roseware@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the name you use on Facebook. We will check and delete anything we hold, and reply to confirm, within 30 days.

Removing the app from your Facebook account

You can also remove the app yourself in Facebook: open Settings, then Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites), choose PerchPoster, and click Remove.

See also our Privacy Policy.
