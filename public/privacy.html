Privacy Policy

PERCH Poster, an app of Perch.mv. Last updated: October 2026.

What this app is

PERCH Poster is a private tool used only by the owner of Perch.mv, an online store in the Maldives. It writes product posts and publishes them on the Perch.mv Facebook Page. It is not offered to the public, and it does not ask visitors to log in.

What information it handles

  • Product information from the Perch.mv store: product names, prices, stock, and photos.
  • A Facebook Page access token for the Perch.mv Page, used only to publish posts to that Page. It is stored as a protected setting on the hosting service and is never shown publicly.

The app does not collect, store, or process personal information about customers, Page visitors, or Facebook users.

How information is used

Product information is used to create post images and captions. To write captions, product details (name, brand, price) are sent to an AI writing service provided by Anthropic. No personal information is included.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information. We share product information only with the services needed to run the app: Facebook (to publish posts), Vercel (hosting), and Anthropic (caption writing).

Your choices

If you believe the app holds information about you, or you want something removed, contact us and we will deal with it promptly. See also our data deletion instructions.
